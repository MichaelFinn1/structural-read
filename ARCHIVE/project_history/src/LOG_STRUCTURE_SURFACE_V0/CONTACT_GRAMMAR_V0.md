## Contact grammar

terrain -> adapter -> observer

Contact is not a property of terrain alone.

Contact arises through the relation between:

- terrain
- adapter
- observer

## Informative examples

OpenStack

terrain:
operational renegotiation

result:
adapter and observer alignment

## Boundary

Do not attribute contact solely to terrain.

Do not attribute non-contact solely to terrain.

Inspect terrain, adapter, and observer jointly.
