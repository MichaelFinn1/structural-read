# RECOVERABILITY_DISCIPLINE_V0

Status: constitutional_note

## Purpose

The workbench prioritizes recoverability over sophistication.

The goal is not:
- maximal interpretation
- hidden-state extraction
- semantic completion
- predictive closure

The goal is:
- lawful reread
- reversible reduction
- provenance continuity
- replayable traversal
- bounded observational movement

## Recoverability properties

The workbench should support:

- future reread without theory reconstruction
- packet replay
- explicit unresolveds
- reversible traversal
- provenance visibility
- branch-local comparison
- graceful weakening
- lawful invalidation

## Current important distinction

The corridor is more important than the labels.

Morphology labels may weaken, split, merge, or revise.

Traversal recoverability must remain stable even when interpretations shift.

## Current hold

Preserve lawful movement first.

Interpretation remains secondary.
