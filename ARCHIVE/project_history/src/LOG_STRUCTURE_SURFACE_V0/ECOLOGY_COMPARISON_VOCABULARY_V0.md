# ECOLOGY_COMPARISON_VOCABULARY_V0

Status: breadth_check_active

Purpose:

The current ecology comparison surface is a breadth-check surface,
not a unified terrain score and not a final taxonomy.

Current observer vocabulary:

stable posture
middle posture
residual posture

residual attachment
largest residual family share
residual forms
rough residual families

These are observational dimensions only.

## Current structural boundaries

Do NOT treat these as:

- anomaly levels
- lifecycle stages
- health scores
- severity levels
- importance rankings
- hidden developmental ladders

The current purpose is only:

compare interacting persistence geometries across terrains.

## Current independent axes

The comparison surface currently exposes at least:

- concentration
- fragmentation
- attachment
- circulation posture

The important observation is that these axes remain independent.

Examples already observed:

high concentration
+
high attachment

high concentration
+
low attachment

high fragmentation
+
partial attachment

high dispersion
+
low attachment

## Current implementation posture

The current labels remain:
- hand-authored
or
- lightly rule-derived

This preserves interpretability and revisability.

Do not aggressively automate labels yet.

## Current hold

Broaden the ecology comparison before deepening it.
Preserve independent ecological dimensions rather than collapsing terrains into unified judgment.
