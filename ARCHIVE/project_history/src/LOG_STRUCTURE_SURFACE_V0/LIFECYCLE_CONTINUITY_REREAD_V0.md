Continuity becomes progressively more compressed as the branch moves toward outcome, coverage, and budgeting layers.

The strongest preserved trajectory remains the RR observer path.

The current branch preserves movement history sufficiently for practical re-entry, but some later artifacts preserve summaries more strongly than trajectories.
