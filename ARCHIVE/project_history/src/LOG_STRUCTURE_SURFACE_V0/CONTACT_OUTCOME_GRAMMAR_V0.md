# CONTACT_OUTCOME_GRAMMAR_V0

Status:
banked

## Purpose

Bank the minimal vocabulary for describing contact-path outcomes.

This is not a classifier.

This is not a score.

This is not a promotion rule.

## Core sequence

contact object
-> contact path
-> path outcome

## Outcome words

survival:

contact remains available across movement

deformation:

contact remains available but measured geometry changes

collapse:

contact disappears under movement

saturation:

contact over-occupies the observer surface or loses useful differentiation

fragmentation:

contact separates into smaller regions under movement

merging:

separate contact regions combine under movement

restraint:

the apparatus does not admit contact

mismatch:

source structure may exist but the adapter or observer does not expose it lawfully

## Boundary

Outcome words describe what happened along a path.

They do not explain why.

They do not assign meaning.

They do not create standing.

They do not choose next moves.
