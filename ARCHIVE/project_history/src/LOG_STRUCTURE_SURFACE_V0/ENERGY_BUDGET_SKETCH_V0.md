# ENERGY_BUDGET_SKETCH_V0

Status:
sketch

## Purpose

Bank a small procedural sketch describing how attention may rise from local contact toward wider review without every glint becoming a packet and without every packet becoming Gardener2 material.

## Core rule

Pressure enters locally.

Energy decides how far attention may rise.

Attention rise is not automatic.

Attention rise is not required.

Non-rise is lawful.

## Energy means

Energy is procedural budget.

Energy is not literal force.

Energy is not intensity.

Energy is not importance.

Energy is not confidence.

Energy is the practical willingness to continue carrying attention beyond the current layer.

## Local contact

Local contact is the default location for attention.

A contact object may be observed, recorded, reread, or closed without rising further.

Most contacts should remain local.

Local handling is a complete and lawful outcome.

## Rise conditions

Attention may rise when one or more of the following persist:

- accumulated pressure
- repeated survival across movement
- unresolved boundary tension
- strong deformation under controlled movement
- failure of local handling
- reappearing contact across paths

Rise is earned through persistence rather than novelty.

Rise is earned through recurrence rather than excitement.

Rise is earned through unresolved pressure rather than immediate interest.

## No-rise conditions

No-rise remains lawful when:

- local handling is sufficient
- pressure dissipates
- contact appears only once
- survival is not repeated
- deformation is minor
- continuation cost exceeds current value
- widening would increase risk without increasing clarity

Novelty is not enough.

Intensity is not enough.

Excitement is not enough.

Theoretical elegance is not enough.

## Stop conditions

Stop is lawful when:

- local handling is complete
- pressure has stabilized
- widening would add complexity without distinction
- continuation cost is disproportionate
- boundary conditions are unclear
- accumulated pressure is insufficient

Pause is lawful.

Unresolved is lawful.

Closure is not required.

## Boundary

Human judgment retains widening authority.

Human judgment retains promotion authority.

Human judgment retains meaning authority.

This sketch does not create:

- automation
- scheduling
- dynamic allocation
- learning
- scoring
- promotion rules
- packet generation
- next-move selection

## Current use

This sketch is a reminder surface.

Its role is to help distinguish:

- local attention
- rising attention
- lawful pause
- lawful stop

without forcing escalation.

## Compression

Pressure enters locally.

Energy governs attention.

Rise is earned.

Non-rise is lawful.

Human judgment remains authoritative.
