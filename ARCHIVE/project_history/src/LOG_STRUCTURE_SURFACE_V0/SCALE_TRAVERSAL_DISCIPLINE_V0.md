## Core distinction

Window size is observer stance, not truth resolution.

A structure does not need to survive identically across scales to remain useful.

Useful scale behavior may include:
- splitting
- merging
- smoothing
- weakening
- persistence
- localization
- partial survivability

## Current learned distinction

Local deformation:
sharp localized recurrence structure visible at fine scale.

Climate persistence:
structure remains partially visible under wider reread without becoming globally dominant.
