# CANDIDATE_DIMENSIONS_REGISTRY_V0

Purpose:

Track possible structural observables that may deserve future promotion
only if they repeatedly survive breadth comparison across terrains.

These are not yet architectural dimensions.

Promotion requirements:

A candidate observable should:
- recur across unlike terrains
- remain structurally independent
- survive grammar stability
- materially change comparative reading
- avoid collapsing existing distinctions

Until then:
treat as observer-side modifiers only.
