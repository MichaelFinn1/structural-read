# ORIENTATION_CONTINUITY_NOT_RECOVERY_V1

Status:
guardrail_banked

## Distinction

Orientation continuity is not continuity recovery.

Orientation continuity means:

- the operator can return
- the prior location is recoverable
- the constitution is known
- the unresolved region remains addressable
- comparison remains possible
- the next packet can be framed lawfully

Continuity recovery means:

- the terrain itself re-stabilizes
- prior structure reconnects
- durable survivability returns
- reintegration becomes admissible

These must not be collapsed.

## Rule

A traversal can succeed even when continuity does not recover.

## Boundary

Do not treat non-recovery as:

- failed investigation
- insufficient processing
- hidden repair waiting to happen
- a reason to force wider synthesis

## Hold

Orientation may survive while continuity returns to fragments.
