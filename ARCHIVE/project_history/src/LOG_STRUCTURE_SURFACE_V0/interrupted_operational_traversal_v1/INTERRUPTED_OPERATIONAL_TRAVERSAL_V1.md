# INTERRUPTED_OPERATIONAL_TRAVERSAL_V1

Status:
phase_open

## Purpose

Test whether operational reread orientation survives interruption, delayed return, constitution shift, and partial replay without forcing continuity restoration.

## Central question

Can investigability remain recoverable even when continuity weakens or fragments?

## Operational pressures

- interrupted investigation
- delayed reread return
- constitution change between visits
- sparse continuity
- local-only survivability
- unresolved disappearance
- weak replay continuity

## Boundary

This phase does not attempt:

- root-cause inference
- semantic anomaly detection
- continuity repair
- automatic stabilization
- forced reintegration

## Hold

Orientation may survive even when durable continuity does not.
