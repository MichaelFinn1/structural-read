## Purpose

## Important correction

Adjacent pair asymmetry does not equal global field asymmetry.

Persistent carrier/background structures may bias local pair dominance counts.

### spacing_symmetry

Do paired intervals preserve similar spacing relationships?

### co_movement_symmetry

Do paired intervals deform together across constitution transitions?

### centerline_symmetry

Do paired intervals preserve approximate symmetry around an implied midpoint or low-activity basin?

### carrier_participation

Does one interval behave more like a persistent background continuity condition than a local active feature?

## Boundary

These are observational balance regimes.

They are not:

- semantic structures
- causal dynamics
- object identities
- true equilibrium measures
