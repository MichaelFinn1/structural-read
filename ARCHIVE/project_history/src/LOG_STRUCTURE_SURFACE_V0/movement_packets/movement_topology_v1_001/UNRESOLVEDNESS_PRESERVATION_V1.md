# UNRESOLVEDNESS_PRESERVATION_V1

Status:
bounded_operational_openness

## Core principle

Unresolvedness is not:
temporary failure.

It is:
lawfully preserved operational openness.

## Purpose

Packets should preserve:
- replayability
- alternative continuations
- widening potential
- later descent
- delayed reread

without forcing:
- semantic closure
- causal narratives
- ontology stabilization

## Operational consequence

Two operators may continue differently
from the same packet
without violating the corridor.

## Important distinction

The system preserves:
movement integrity

not:
interpretive certainty.

## Boundary

This architecture does not optimize toward:
- final explanation
- hidden truth extraction
- universal interpretation
