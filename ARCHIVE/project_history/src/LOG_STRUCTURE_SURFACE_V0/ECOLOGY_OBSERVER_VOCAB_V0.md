# ECOLOGY_OBSERVER_VOCAB_V0

Purpose:

Lightweight observer vocabulary for describing persistence geometries.

This vocabulary is descriptive only.

It is not:
- ranking
- anomaly labeling
- ontology
- classification
- lifecycle inference

## Stable posture vocabulary

concentrated
=
large recurrence mass concentrated in a small number of structures

distributed
=
recurrence mass spread across many structures

mixed
=
no strongly dominant concentration posture

## Middle posture vocabulary

circulating
=
reuse spreads broadly across contexts without strong anchoring

weak_reuse
=
limited contextual reuse structure observed

mixed
=
no dominant contextual reuse posture

## Residual posture vocabulary

attached_clouds
=
singleton exact forms cluster into small rough-shape families with high local attachment

fragmented
=
many small residual families with weak dominant concentration

detached_clouds
=
strong rough-family concentration with weak local attachment

isolated_dispersion
=
residual forms broadly dispersed with weak concentration and weak attachment

fragmented_attached
=
fragmented residual ecology with moderate or high local attachment

## Boundary

These are observer summaries only.

Terrains may exhibit multiple postures simultaneously.

No posture implies:
- importance
- severity
- anomaly
- health
- progression
- maturity
