# BENCH_HISTORIAN_V0

Status: PROVISIONAL

## Purpose

Define a future bounded continuity utility.

The Bench Historian preserves continuity.

It does not own the work.

Allowed:

- build timelines
- link scripts to outputs
- list unresolveds
- generate reentry notes

Not allowed:

- begin investigations
- widen admissible space
- promote distinctions
- assign meaning
