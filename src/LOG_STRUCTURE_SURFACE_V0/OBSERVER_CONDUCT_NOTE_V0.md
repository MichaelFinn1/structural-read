# OBSERVER_CONDUCT_NOTE_V0

Status: active_method_note

## Purpose

This note stabilizes how the observer is allowed to move between layers.

The current work is no longer only asking:

can surfaces expose structure?

It is now also asking:

can the observer move between layers without distortion, inflation, or collapse?

## Core conduct rule

Higher reads are allowed only while they remain descendable.

Lower evidence is allowed to weaken, narrow, or revise higher reads.

A read survives reread when it becomes more precise without becoming more forceful.

## Healthy reread behavior

A healthy reread may produce:

- preservation
- weakening
- refinement
- unresolvedness
- conditional survival
- stop

A healthy reread does not require:

- confirmation
- rejection
- explanation
- semantic closure
- prediction
- causality

## Graceful weakening

Graceful weakening means:

a higher read contacts lower evidence,
loses overreach,
keeps what remains structurally supported,
and returns in narrower form.

Example:

from:
braided terrain

to:
persistent local braided coupling regions within a still-structured terrain

## Partial survivability

Reads do not have to resolve into true / false.

A read may survive partially if:
- some structure holds
- some scope narrows
- some uncertainty remains
- descent remains possible

## Observer permissions

The observer may:

- reduce
- expose
- compare
- reread
- descend
- refine
- bank
- stop

The observer may not:

- infer hidden state
- assign cause
- declare anomaly
- collapse adjacency into belonging
- collapse recurrence into identity
- convert usefulness into authority
- automate interpretation

## Current hold

Observer conduct is lawful when it increases structural legibility while preserving reversibility, locality, and incomplete access to the originating system.
