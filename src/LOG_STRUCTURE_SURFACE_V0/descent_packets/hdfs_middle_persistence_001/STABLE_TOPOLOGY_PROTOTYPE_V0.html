Stable Topology Prototype V0

Sandbox prototype only. This page studies stable recurrence topology before compressing it into the overview terrain strip. Stable means templates occurring five or more times: recurring enough to have shape. Stable does not mean normal, safe, boring, ignorable, or explained. This page shows recurrence posture, not importance.
