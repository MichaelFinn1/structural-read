# PAUSE_BASIN_SURFACE_V1

Status:
observer_side_pause_hold_surface

## Purpose

This surface exposes pause-like behavior as procedural evidence.

Pause is not inactivity.

Pause means non-forced continuation, low deformation pressure, unresolved survivability, or stable reread hold.

## Pause types

spacing_hold
continuity_hold
unresolved_hold

## Boundary

This surface does not infer intent, meaning, or hidden state.

It records conditions where closure should remain delayed or bounded.

## Hold

Pause preserves admissibility when forced continuation would damage replayability.
