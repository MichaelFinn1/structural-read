# CONSTITUTIONAL_REREAD_INVARIANTS_V1

Status:
active_replay_floor

## Purpose

This note banks the current operational invariants that protect lawful reread movement.

It is not a theory note.
It is a replay floor.

## Invariants

1. Explicit constitutions

Every reread declares its observational constitution.

2. Replay provenance

Every higher read should retain a recoverable path back to its supporting surfaces.

3. Lawful pause

Pause is a valid stabilizing action when closure would damage replayability.

4. Admissibility differentiation

Exposure, support, admission, and promotion remain distinct.

5. Bounded reread

Rereads are local, declared, and reversible.

6. Reversible traversal

Movement across scales should preserve return paths.

7. Non-sovereign scales

No window, layer, or constitution is treated as final truth.

8. Unresolved survivability

Ambiguity may be preserved when evidence does not justify closure.

9. Replay-relative recursion

Recursive descent or widening remains selectable, not mandatory.

10. Replay inspectability

The conduct of observation remains observable.

## Boundary

These invariants do not define ontology.

They protect lawful movement through partially unresolved terrain.

## Hold

Harden lawful reread conduct before enriching interpretation.
