# REPLAY_DEGRADATION_SURFACE_V1

Status:
observer_side_replay_degradation_surface

## Purpose

This surface exposes where reread movement weakens, relocates, or loses recoverability.

It does not judge correctness.

It records degradation pressure under replay variation.

## Degradation types

transition_loss
signature_drift
candidate_survivability_gap
provenance_break
spacing_collapse
forced_resolution_pressure

## Boundary

Replay degradation is not failure.

It is evidence about where movement becomes less lawful or less recoverable.

## Hold

Learn from replay weakening before strengthening interpretation.
