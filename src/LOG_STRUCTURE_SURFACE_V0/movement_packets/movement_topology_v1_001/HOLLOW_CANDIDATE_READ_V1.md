# HOLLOW_CANDIDATE_READ_V1

Status:
observer_side_hollow_candidate_surface

## Purpose

This surface exposes candidate hollow-related organizational behavior under declared reread constitutions.

It does not detect true hollows.

It only exposes replayable evidence surfaces related to:

- spacing preservation
- low-transition regions
- carrier/background separation
- recomposition corridors
- constitution-local gaps

## Core rule

A hollow is not a thing.

A hollow is a candidate low-participation or spacing-preserving relation under a declared constitution.

## Candidate types

spacing_basin:
persistent spacing across low-participation regions

low_transition_basin:
reduced transition density under reread

carrier_separation_basin:
persistent carrier/background separation candidate

recomposition_corridor:
merge/split density under constitution transition

constitution_local_gap:
gap visible only under local constitutions

## Boundary

This surface does not infer:

- true centers
- latent topology
- semantic voids
- hidden attractors
- universal field laws
- object identity

## Hold

Expose candidate hollow evidence first.

Delay topology claims until replay survivability is earned.
