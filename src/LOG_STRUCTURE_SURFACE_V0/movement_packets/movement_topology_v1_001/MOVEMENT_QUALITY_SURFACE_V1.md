# MOVEMENT_QUALITY_SURFACE_V1

Status:
observer_side_movement_quality_surface

## Purpose

This surface summarizes reread movement quality across existing constitutional hardening surfaces.

It does not judge interpretation quality.

It asks whether movement remains inspectable, replayable, and bounded.

## Movement dimensions

transition_texture:
how adjacent constitutions deform relations

degradation_pressure:
where replay movement weakens or relocates

pause_support:
where lawful non-forcing is procedurally supported

basin_survivability:
whether candidate spacing basins survive replay variation

## Boundary

Movement quality is not truth quality.

Movement quality is not semantic correctness.

Movement quality describes whether reread conduct remains recoverable under deformation.

## Hold

Optimize for lawful movement before interpretive richness.
