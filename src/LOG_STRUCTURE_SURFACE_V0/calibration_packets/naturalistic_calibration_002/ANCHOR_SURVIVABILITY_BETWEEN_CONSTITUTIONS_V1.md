## Purpose

This surface tracks which residual-band centers remain positionally close across adjacent constitutions.

It does not infer identity.

It only asks whether a visible anchor position survives reread deformation.

## What motivated it

The visual read suggested:

- 175 begins losing boundary packet clarity
- 200 becomes ladder-like
- 200 to 250 preserves several static bridge points
- 300 / 350 / 400 appear to let different anchor regions hold the field in turn
- 500 to 1000 consolidates toward broad recomposition

## What it measures

- adjacent constitution pair
- nearest band center relation
- center delta
- width delta
- anchor_holds / near_reposition / reconstitutes_elsewhere

## Boundary

Anchor survival is not object survival.

A stable center does not prove the same feature persists.

It only marks a position where recurrence organization remains close under reread.

## Hold

Track anchor survival across constitutions before interpreting bridge behavior.
