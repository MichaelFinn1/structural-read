# CADENCE_SURVIVABILITY_SURFACE_V1

Status:
observer_side_spacing_surface

## Purpose

This surface tracks spacing cadence continuity across constitutions.

It does not track packet identity.

It tracks whether interval organization survives widening.

## What it measures

- dominant repeated gap
- cadence strength
- cadence breaks
- break locations
- deformation onset region

## Important distinction

Packet morphology may deform while cadence survives.

This surface treats spacing continuity as distinct from packet symmetry.

## Boundary

This surface does not infer:

- object persistence
- hidden cause
- semantic structure
- true temporal process
- universal rhythm law

All observations remain replay-relative and constitution-relative.

## Hold

Spacing cadence may survive longer than exact packet morphology.
