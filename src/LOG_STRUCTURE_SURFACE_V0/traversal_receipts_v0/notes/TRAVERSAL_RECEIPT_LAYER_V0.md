# TRAVERSAL_RECEIPT_LAYER_V0

Status:
manual_traversal_memory_layer

## Purpose

This layer preserves neighboring-constitution movement memory.

It records what movement happened, what the observer noticed, and what changed across transition.

It does not recommend movement.

It does not score lenses.

It does not infer meaning.

## Core invariant

The observer remains between:

- outer conditioning constitution
- inner articulated constitution

The system records traversal provenance.

The human preserves continuity adjudication.

## Receipt role

Traversal receipts preserve:

- terrain
- replay
- blue constitution
- green constitution
- local position
- movement direction
- operator marker
- observed relation
- optional note

## Boundary

This layer is append-only.

It is not a classifier, optimizer, recommender, or authority layer.

## Hold

Accumulate traversal memory, not structural conclusions.
