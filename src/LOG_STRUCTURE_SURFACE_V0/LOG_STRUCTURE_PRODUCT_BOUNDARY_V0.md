# LOG_STRUCTURE_PRODUCT_BOUNDARY_V0.md

Status: product_boundary_note

## Product identity

Log Structure Surface V0 is a pre-interpretive log reduction surface.

It is not:

- anomaly detection
- security monitoring
- observability platform
- incident response
- predictive scoring
- root-cause analysis

## It exposes

- repeated line templates
- stable recurrence
- middle recurrence
- residual lines
- sample lines
- structural compression

## It preserves

- uncertainty
- human judgment
- unresolvedness
- auditability
- raw samples

## Boundary

Repeated does not mean important.

Residual does not mean anomalous.

Middle does not mean suspicious.

These are structural classes only.

## One-line hold

Structure is surfaced; operational meaning is not assigned.
